Basics

  • Enemies march from the red portals to your core. Each one that gets through costs lives.
  • Pick a tower from the dock, then click/tap an empty tile to build. On touch, tap once to preview, again to confirm.
  • Click a tower to upgrade it, change its targeting, or sell it (70% refund, 100% within 3s of building).
  • Call the next wave early for bonus credits.
